Harbor Data Services — counsel working assessment
Matter LEG-COUNSEL-55N8; prepared 23 September for internal contract negotiation.
The continuity clause currently refers to backup completion, while the customer schedule expects a measured restoration exercise. Our draft negotiation position is to narrow the remedy to documented direct recovery expense and offer a service credit of up to AUD 18000 rather than broad consequential-loss exposure. The account team has not yet shared this negotiating range with the customer. Counsel will revisit the wording after the October exercise provides measured recovery times. The working assessment is part of the legal matter file, separate from the supplier assurance evidence pack.
